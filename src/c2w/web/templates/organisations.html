This creates an encryption key

Each organisation's credentials are sealed under a key of its own, so one company's stored tokens cannot be read with another's. The key is wrapped by the platform master key — if that is ever lost, so is every credential for every organisation.

Removing one is not offered here

Deleting an organisation would take its calls, recordings and audit trail with it, and those are the records this platform exists to keep. If a company really has to go, that is a deliberate job for the command line with a backup taken first.

CommPeak accounts

Read-only sources. One recordings bucket, and its call records, per account.

Adding an account

Credentials are entered on the server so they are never typed into a browser, and are encrypted before they are stored:

c2w-admin connection add --brand <org> --tenant <tenant> --name "…" --bucket <uuid>

CommPeak also requires this server's public address on the account's access list — that is the most common reason a new account fails.
